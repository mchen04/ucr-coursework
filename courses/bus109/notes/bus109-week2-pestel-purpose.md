# BUS 109 Week 2 - PESTEL and Purpose

Related: [[2026-04-09]], [[ashley-grice]]

## Durable anchors

- Trend analysis = scanning, monitoring, and evaluating the external environment for threats and opportunities.
- PESTEL breaks the environment into political, economic, sociocultural, technological, ecological, and legal forces.
- PESTEL is useful because it is simple, cost-effective, and comprehensive, but it can oversimplify and becomes hard to turn into action when factors are ambiguous or fast-changing.
- Ashley Grice's TED talk framed purpose as a timeless organizational "why," distinct from mission and vision.
- The Delta almond note worked as the concrete example of purpose becoming muscle memory across the front line.

## Framework reminders

- Political: rules, regulation, mandates, political mood, lobbying, litigation.
- Economic: interest rates, inflation, exchange rates, labor conditions, economic growth.
- Sociocultural: norms, values, demographics, immigration, changing consumer behavior.
- Technological: AI, breakthrough innovation, process innovation.
- Ecological: climate change, disasters, sustainability, resource scarcity.
- Legal: laws, court decisions, patents, compliance costs, indirect legal effects.

## Purpose reminders

- Mission = what you do.
- Vision = where you are headed.
- Purpose = why the organization exists.
- Good purpose should be authentic, excavated from inside out, and applicable across the whole organization.
