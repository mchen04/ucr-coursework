# Boeing 737 MAX

## Durable takeaways

- Safety is the competitive moat in aviation; engineering judgment should outrank management pressure.
- Customer threats can distort strategy, but Boeing likely had more leverage in the duopoly than it used.
- The FAA relationship was a long-term asset; hiding MCAS changes traded short-term speed for lasting trust loss.
- The slide-ready fourth point was: "Ground after crash one, not crash two." Lion Air was a 5-month warning; Boeing kept flying instead of grounding voluntarily after October 2018.
