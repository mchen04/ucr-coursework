# BUS146 Zappos Case

## Durable takeaways

- Zappos' core strategy was customer-experience differentiation, not price: free shipping, free returns, and service as the moat.
- The assignment framed free shipping / returns and Holacracy as the two main assumptions worth testing.
- Free shipping / returns was effectively a company-wide, no-control-group test with no clean benchmark or success threshold.
- Holacracy was also rolled out company-wide, which made its effects hard to separate from the concurrent Supercloud migration.
