# BUS146 MVP Sprint

## Idea

`Lined Up`: a paid service for student founders that books 5 customer discovery calls within 72 hours.

## Durable takeaways

- The pitch worked best when framed around founders *not* doing outreach themselves, not around founders being unable to do it.
- The strongest concern was reach quality for niche or B2B targets.
- A screening step helps, but broad consumer audiences are the practical MVP scope.
